\begin{abstract}
\noindent\textsc{CellAveragePBE} is a Julia package that solves a size-structured population
balance for a growing, dividing yeast population, coupled to a well-mixed bioreactor chemistry
model. The population balance is a mesoscopic integro-partial-differential equation discretized with
the Cell Average Technique for division and a first-order upwind flux for growth; the chemistry is a
stiff system of eight ordinary differential equations. The fully coupled model reproduces the
sustained metabolic oscillations characteristic of the Henson yeast system. This document describes
the mathematical model, the numerical methods, the software architecture, and the public API.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textit{The complete source is at \url{https://github.com/Kyle-Hawkins/CellAveragePBE} (MIT).}
\end{abstract}

\section{Overview}
The package integrates the coupled system
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial N(x,t)}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial\,[\,g\,N(x,t)\,]}{\partial x}
   = \underbrace{B_{\mathrm{div}}(x,t)}_{\text{division birth}}
   - \underbrace{\big[\,S(x) + D\,\big]\,N(x,t)}_{\text{division + dilution loss}},
\label{eq:pbe}
\end{equation}
for the cell number density $N(x,t)$ over cell mass $x$, together with eight ordinary differential
equations for the liquid- and gas-phase chemistry (glucose, ethanol, dissolved and gas-phase oxygen
and carbon dioxide, and two ``effective'' sensed concentrations). Here $g$ is the mass-specific
growth rate, $S(x)$ the division (selection) rate, and $D$ the dilution rate. The growth rate and
division kernels depend on the local chemistry, closing the coupling.

\section{Mathematical model}

\subsection{Division kernels}
A dividing mother of mass $y$ produces daughters distributed according to the breakage kernel
\begin{equation}
b(x,y) = A\Big[\,e^{-\beta (x - m_t)^2} + e^{-\beta (x - (y - m_t))^2}\,\Big],
\qquad x \le y,\; y > m_t + m_0,
\end{equation}
two Gaussian lobes centered on the transition mass $m_t$ and its complement $y - m_t$. The division
rate is set by the selection kernel
\begin{equation}
S(x) = \gamma\, e^{-\varepsilon (x - m_d)^2}\,\mathbb{1}[\,m_t - m_0 \le x \le m_d\,]
      + \gamma\,\mathbb{1}[\,x > m_d\,],
\end{equation}
a Gaussian rising to a plateau above the division mass $m_d$. The masses $m_t(S)$ and $m_d(S)$ are
piecewise-linear functions of the local substrate concentration $S = G_{\mathrm{eff}} + E_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

\subsection{Growth kinetics}
The mass-specific growth rate is a sum of three Monod modes (fermentative glucose uptake, oxidative
glucose uptake, oxidative ethanol uptake with glucose inhibition):
\begin{align}
K_{gf}(G) &= \mu_{gf}\frac{G}{K_{gf}+G}, &
K_{go}(G,O) &= \mu_{go}\frac{G}{K_{go}+G}\frac{O}{K_{gd}+O}, \\
K_{eo}(G,E,O) &= \mu_{eo}\frac{E}{K_{eo}+E}\frac{O}{K_{ed}+O}\frac{K_i}{K_i+G}, &
g &= K_{gf} + K_{go} + K_{eo}.
\end{align}
All concentration arguments are clamped at zero before entering the saturation terms, which keeps the
rates physical (they vanish as a substrate is depleted) and the coupled ODE stable.

\subsection{Bioreactor chemistry}
The eight chemistry states evolve by feed/dilution, gas--liquid mass transfer, and metabolic demand
integrated over the population, e.g.\ for glucose
\begin{equation}
\frac{dG}{dt} = D\,(G_f - G) - \int N(x)\Big(\tfrac{K_{gf}}{Y_{gf}} + \tfrac{K_{go}}{Y_{go}}\Big)\,dx,
\end{equation}
with analogous balances for ethanol, oxygen, and carbon dioxide. The effective concentrations relax
toward the bulk values with a first-order sensing lag, $\dot G_{\mathrm{eff}} = \alpha_g (G - G_{\mathrm{eff}})$.

\section{Numerical methods}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em, style=nextline]
\item[Division (integro-PDE).] The birth term is a double integral over dividing mothers. All
newborns produced within a cell are first averaged into a single representative mass
$\bar v = V_b/B_b$, and that average is then redistributed onto the two neighboring grid nodes ---
the \emph{Cell Average Technique} of Kumar et al.~\cite{kumar2006cellaverage,kumar2008efficient} ---
which conserves both the number and the
mass placed on the coarse grid. Averaging before redistributing is what separates this scheme from
the earlier \emph{fixed pivot} technique~\cite{kumar1996fixedpivot}, which splits each birth across its bracketing nodes
individually and so discards the information about where inside the cell the births concentrated;
on coarse grids that discarding over-predicts the number density in the large-mass tail. The inner
breakage integrals are evaluated with adaptive Gauss--Kronrod quadrature
(\texttt{QuadGK}), pruned by skipping cells with zero division rate.
\item[Growth (advection).] Cell growth is a first-order \emph{upwind} finite-volume flux
$-\partial(gN)/\partial x$ that advects cells up the mass axis.
\item[Coupled integration.] The full state (the $n$ population cells plus eight chemistry ODEs) is
advanced with \texttt{FBDF}, a variable-order BDF method, using a finite-difference Jacobian --- the
non-smooth division kernels are not amenable to automatic differentiation.
\item[Stability.] Clamping the substrate/oxygen concentrations at zero inside the kinetics keeps the
fast startup transient physical and the stiff solve well-posed.
\end{description}

\subsection{\texttt{src/CellAveragePBE.jl}}
The top-level module. It loads the dependencies (\texttt{QuadGK} for quadrature, \texttt{OrdinaryDiffEq}
for time integration, and \texttt{ADTypes} for the finite-difference Jacobian specifier),
\texttt{include}s the component files in dependency order, and \texttt{export}s the public API --- the
parameter, grid, and state types, the kernels and kinetics, and the solve drivers.

\lstinputlisting{../src/CellAveragePBE.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{src/parameters.jl}}
Defines \texttt{PBEParameters}, an immutable \texttt{Base.@kwdef} struct holding every physical and
numerical constant: the Monod kinetic parameters, the piecewise-linear division-mass constants, the
breakage/selection/ethanol kernel shape parameters, the growth regularizers, and the bioreactor-chemistry
constants. Collecting all constants in one keyword-constructed struct keeps them out of the function
bodies and makes the model reproducible and easy to perturb --- any subset
is overridden by keyword, e.g.\ \texttt{PBEParameters(D = 0.3)}.

\lstinputlisting{../src/parameters.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{src/grid.jl}}
Defines \texttt{MassGrid}, a uniform mass grid storing the $n+1$ cell edges (\texttt{bounds}) and the
$n$ cell midpoints (\texttt{centers}); cell $i$ occupies $[\,\texttt{bounds}[i],\,\texttt{bounds}[i{+}1]\,]$.
It also provides two helpers used by the Cell Average Technique: \texttt{p\_upper}, the upper integration
limit for daughters of a given mother cell, and \texttt{lambda\_weight}, the linear interpolation weight
that redistributes a newborn onto a neighboring node.

\lstinputlisting{../src/grid.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{src/kernels.jl}}
The population-balance kernels, written as plain branchy Julia (replacing the original
Heaviside-based lambdas). \texttt{m\_ts} and \texttt{m\_ds} give the transition and division masses as
continuous piecewise-linear functions of substrate. \texttt{breakage} is the two-Gaussian daughter-mass
distribution (gated so daughters are lighter than the mother and only sufficiently large mothers divide);
\texttt{selection} is the division-rate kernel; \texttt{ethanol\_formation} is the mass-specific
ethanol-production weight.

\lstinputlisting{../src/kernels.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{src/kinetics.jl}}
The Monod growth kinetics: \texttt{K\_gf}, \texttt{K\_go}, \texttt{K\_eo} for the three metabolic modes
and \texttt{K\_net} for their sum. Each concentration argument passes through $\texttt{pos}(x)=\max(x,0)$
before entering a saturation term, so a transient overshoot to a slightly negative oxygen or glucose
concentration cannot make an $O(10^{-3})$-affinity denominator blow up --- the rates simply vanish as a
substrate is depleted, keeping the coupled stiff solve well-posed.

\lstinputlisting{../src/kinetics.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{src/cellaverage.jl}}
Implements the Cell Average Technique for the division birth term. \texttt{breakage\_births} computes,
once per right-hand-side evaluation, the number- and mass-birth into every cell as a sum over dividing
mother cells (each inner integral by adaptive Gauss--Kronrod quadrature, with zero-rate cells skipped to
prune most of the nominal $O(n^2)$ work). \texttt{birth\_CA} then redistributes those newborns onto each
interior node: it forms the average newborn mass $\bar v = V/B$ in the cell and its two neighbors and
splits each contribution onto the node with linear weights, gated by where $\bar v$ falls, so that number
and mass are placed consistently on the coarse grid.

\lstinputlisting{../src/cellaverage.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{src/chemistry.jl}}
Defines the \texttt{ChemState} container for the eight chemistry species, a composite-trapezoid
integrator \texttt{trapz} for integrating metabolic demand over the population, and
\texttt{chemistry\_derivs}, which returns the eight time derivatives: feed/dilution and gas--liquid mass
transfer balanced against the population's substrate uptake and product formation, plus the first-order
relaxation of the effective (sensed) concentrations.

\lstinputlisting{../src/chemistry.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{src/model.jl}}
Assembles and solves the coupled system. \texttt{pbe\_ode\_term} gives the net rate for one interior cell
--- division birth (from \texttt{cellaverage.jl}) plus the first-order upwind growth-advection flux, minus
dilution and division loss. \texttt{pbe\_rhs!} is the in-place right-hand side of the full $n+8$ state
(population cells with fixed end caps, followed by the chemistry). \texttt{initial\_state} builds the
bimodal initial distribution and chemistry vector; \texttt{solve\_pbe} constructs the \texttt{ODEProblem}
and integrates it with a stiff BDF solver (the \texttt{N\_scale} keyword sets the
cell-number-to-concentration coupling factor). \texttt{pbe\_population\_rhs!} and \texttt{solve\_population}
provide the population-only variant in a fixed chemical environment, and \texttt{split\_state} unpacks a
solution vector into \texttt{(N, ChemState)}.

\lstinputlisting{../src/model.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{test/runtests.jl}}
The 26-test suite. It checks grid construction and the edge/center invariants; continuity of the
division-mass functions at their breakpoints and the gating of the breakage, selection, and
ethanol-formation kernels; saturation and positivity of the Monod kinetics; the trapezoidal integrator
against known integrals; and that the assembled right-hand side is finite, correctly sized, and holds the
boundary cells fixed.

\lstinputlisting{../test/runtests.jl}

\subsection{\texttt{examples/demo.jl}}
A minimal, dependency-light script that solves the coupled model and the population-only model and prints
summary diagnostics --- a quick check that the package runs end to end.

\lstinputlisting{../examples/demo.jl}

\section{Public API}

\paragraph{Types.}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em, style=nextline]
\item[\texttt{PBEParameters}] Immutable, keyword-constructed struct holding every model constant
(kinetics, division thresholds, kernels, chemistry). Override any subset by keyword, e.g.
\texttt{PBEParameters(D = 0.3)}.
\item[\texttt{MassGrid(n; xmax)}] A uniform mass grid of \texttt{n} cells on $[0,\texttt{xmax}]$,
storing cell edges and midpoints.
\item[\texttt{ChemState}] The eight chemistry states $(G, G_{\mathrm{eff}}, E, E_{\mathrm{eff}}, O, O_{\mathrm{out}}, C, C_{\mathrm{out}})$.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Drivers.}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em, style=nextline]
\item[\texttt{solve\_pbe(; n, tspan, p, N\_scale, \ldots)}] Build and integrate the fully coupled
population + chemistry system. Returns \texttt{(sol, grid, p)}; each solution state has length
$n + 8$. \texttt{N\_scale} sets the cell-number-to-concentration coupling factor.
\item[\texttt{solve\_population(; n, tspan, env, N0, \ldots)}] Integrate the population balance alone
in a fixed chemical environment \texttt{env}; useful for isolating the growth/division numerics.
\item[\texttt{split\_state(u, n)}] Split a solution state vector into \texttt{(N, ChemState)}.
\end{description}

\paragraph{Kernels and kinetics.} \texttt{m\_ts}, \texttt{m\_ds}, \texttt{breakage},
\texttt{selection}, \texttt{ethanol\_formation}, \texttt{K\_gf}, \texttt{K\_go}, \texttt{K\_eo},
\texttt{K\_net} are all exported for inspection and plotting.

\section{Usage}
\begin{lstlisting}
using Pkg; Pkg.activate("."); Pkg.instantiate()
using CellAveragePBE

# Coupled bioreactor -- sustained metabolic oscillations
out = solve_pbe(n = 60, tspan = (0.0, 25.0), p = PBEParameters(D = 0.3), N_scale = 1/500)
N, chem = split_state(out.sol.u[end], out.grid.n)

# Population balance alone, in a fixed chemical environment
outp = solve_population(n = 80, tspan = (0.0, 4.0))
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Testing}
The suite in \texttt{test/runtests.jl} (26 tests) verifies grid construction, continuity and gating of
the kernels, saturation of the Monod kinetics, the trapezoidal integrator, and well-formedness of the
right-hand side. Run it with:
\begin{lstlisting}
julia --project=. -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.test()'
\end{lstlisting}
